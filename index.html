How to play

Place the three pieces from the tray onto the 10 by 10 board. Pieces cannot be rotated.

Fill a whole row or column and it clears. Rows and columns that fill at the same time clear together.

You score 1 point per placed cell and 10 points per cleared line. When all three pieces are placed, you get three new ones.

The game ends when none of the pieces you hold fits anywhere on the board.

Controls

  • Mouse or touch: drag a piece onto the board. On touch screens the piece floats above your finger.
  • Tap or click: pick a piece (or its 1, 2, 3 button), then tap the board cell for the piece's top-left corner.
  • Keyboard: 1 to 3 pick a piece, arrow keys move it, Enter or Space places it, Escape cancels or pauses.
  • Pause: the pause button beside the score, or Escape.
  • Sound: if your browser keeps the game silent, the speaker button beside the score turns sound on. The game is fully playable without sound.
