## Files

| File | Why |
|---|---|
| `src/domain/clearing/clearing.ts` | `resolveClears(board)` scans every row and column on the input board first. It then clears the union in a copy and returns `completedRows`, `completedColumns` and the unique `cleared` cells in row-major order. An unchanged board is returned as-is when nothing is complete |
| `tests/domain/clearing.test.ts` | Before/after grid fixtures with occupancy counts |

## Checks

- **One row × one column:** lines `[2]` / `[2]`, 19 unique cells cleared, and the unrelated tile stays where it was.

- **Full board:** 20 lines and 100 cells cleared.
- **Not cleared:** a row with a gap stays, and same-colored tiles don't match. Nothing falls (no gravity). A second call finds no lines, so nothing cascades.
- **Other boards:** a rectangular 7×5 board works, the frozen input is unchanged, and occupancy after = occupancy before − unique cleared cells holds in every fixture.
