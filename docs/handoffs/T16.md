What the tests prove:
- **Commit before animation:** the seed-2026 recording is replayed through slot buttons and board clicks up to move 17, which clears row 4, with the clear fade slowed to 2000 ms (theme maximum; cosmetic, outside the gameplay hash). Right after the click:
  - the score already shows the committed 76 and `data-state` is `resolving` (`docs/evidence/t16/clear-fade-*.png`);
  - a command during resolving is not accepted.
- **Hidden tab:** `data-state=paused`, Resume is focused, and over 4 s `requestAnimationFrame` is called at most once more (counted by an init script). Before the fix it kept running at about 60 per second through Pixi's `Ticker.system`.
- **Resume:** the same clear continues (`resolving`) and finishes. The score stays 76, row 4 is empty afterwards, and the next recorded move scores exactly once.
- **Blur and Escape:** blur pauses and makes the canvas inert; Enter on Resume returns focus to the board; Escape first clears a selection, then pauses.
- **Reduced motion:** the inter-cell gap next to a freshly placed tile is covered by the pulse normally and stays background under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- **Teardown:** an init script tracks live listeners on every target, including AbortSignal removal. Five Retry restarts, each with feedback or a selection in flight, leave the count at the baseline of 34 every time, with no page errors. Before the fixes it grew by 3 per restart (slot buttons).
- **Stale frame after Resume (test race, not a defect):** under full parallel load, the pointer blur/hidden tests once read the last frame drawn before the pause (the cancelled drag's ghost). Rendering is stopped while paused, so the scene was already clear but not yet redrawn. The tests now wait two frames after Resume. 30/30 passed with 3 repeats in parallel.
- **Node:** the pulse and fade timings, the reduced-motion opacity cue, the 0.1 s dt cap, frozen-while-paused, the reset clock after Resume, one completion only, zero-length skip, and an unchanged committed state at 1/144 s steps.
