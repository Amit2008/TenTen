## Behavior changed

- **Font:** all HTML text is Atkinson Hyperlegible (Braille Institute, designed for low-vision readability). Its zero has a slash ("Score 0" shows a slashed 0). That is the font's own design, not a rendering fault.
- **High contrast on the board:** black tiles on light-gray cells, white background, in the board, the tray and the floating piece. Occupancy no longer depends on hue: tile against empty cell is 14.9:1.
- **Pause button:** beside the score, 44×44 CSS px. Before this, touch players could not pause or reach the main menu except by leaving the tab. The GDD lists a pause button icon as a required asset (7.3).
- **Buttons:** the menu, pause, results and dialog buttons now share the slot-button look instead of the browser's gray default.
- **Status line:** "Renderer ready (WebGL2). Content 1.1.0 loaded." no longer takes two lines above the score on phones. Loading… and errors still show.

## Contrast (WCAG 2.x relative luminance, computed with a script)

High contrast board. The outline, cross and ring are drawn over both empty cells and black tiles, so each needs 3:1 against both:

| Color | On white | On empty `#D9D9D9` | On tile `#000000` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tile `#000000` | 21:1 | 14.9:1 | n/a |
| Line outline / selection `#005FCC` | 6.0:1 | 4.2:1 | 3.5:1 |
| Invalid cross `#C8102E` | 5.9:1 | 4.2:1 | 3.6:1 |
| Anchor ring `#8A2BE2` | 6.0:1 | 4.2:1 | 3.5:1 |

Light and dark tiles against empty cells (decorative colors, unchanged from T19a):

| Tile | Light (`#DDDCD4`) | Dark (`#2E3A3C`) |
|---|---|---|
| Teal `#247D80` | 3.5:1 | 2.4:1 |
| Amber `#CC8A36` | 2.1:1 | 4.1:1 |
| Violet `#7D65A8` | 3.6:1 | 2.4:1 |
| Coral `#CD6958` | 2.7:1 | 3.2:1 |
| Green `#598B51` | 2.9:1 | 2.9:1 |

No single tile color reaches 3:1 against both the light and the dark empty cell (it would need a luminance of at most 0.20 and at least 0.21). So light and dark keep their colors, and High contrast is the mode for players who need occupancy without hue. Validity cues never rely on color: invalid uses an outline and a cross, completed lines an outline on every cell, and the selection a slot frame and an anchor ring.

## Defects and decisions

- **Font choice:** Atkinson Hyperlegible over a decorative face. Readability is the GDD's visual priority (7.1), it is OFL, and the two weights in use cost 35 kB. Only the Latin subset ships; other characters fall back to system fonts.
- **Font wait cap of 3 s:** startup should not hang on a font. If it arrives later, `font-display: swap` shows it and the new `fitToHost` observers resize the board.
- **High contrast uses one tile color:** occupancy and shape are what the player must read. Five hues on white cannot all reach 3:1 against an empty cell, and black can.
- **Pause icon inline:** inline SVG with `fill: currentColor` follows all three themes with no extra request. `public/content/icons` stays empty.
- **Test fixes, not app defects:**
  - The integration scroll control swiped on the status line, which is now visually hidden; it swipes on the score row (also HTML).
  - The keyboard-scroll test read `scrollY` immediately after ArrowDown. It used to pass only because the taller page had already scrolled when the canvas took focus. It now waits for the smooth scroll.
