## Checks

- `npm run typecheck`: 0 errors.
- `npx vitest run`: 112 passed overall, 44 of them placement tests (`docs/evidence/t05/placement-tests.txt`).
- **Borders:** all 19 shapes are legal at the four extreme anchors and illegal one step past every edge, at negative anchors and far outside.
- **Independent count:** legal anchors on the empty board equal (11 − width) × (11 − height) for every shape.
- **Overlap:** for every shape, occupying any single target cell rejects the whole placement. Neighbouring cells don't block.
- **Empty origin:** `corner_2_2` anchors at its bounding box, and its empty (0,0) may sit over an occupied cell. Negative anchors are still rejected when a real cell would leave the board.
- **Purity:** frozen inputs stay unchanged, and anchors of 0.5 and NaN are rejected.
