The runner provides two functions:
- **`replay(config, seed, commands)`** returns the canonical state after every move.
- **`randomPlay(config, seed, maxMoves, policy)`** plays legal moves through the real `GameSession`. Choices come from a separate test-only xorshift stream, never the deal generator. About one in four turns it also injects an invalid command (stale session, stale revision, unknown instance, out of bounds, overlap) and requires a rejection with no effect. After every accepted move it checks, using independent reimplementations:
  - Completed rows and columns are recomputed on the pre-clear board.
  - Cleared cells are exactly the unique union of those lines.
  - Every other cell is either the placed tile or unchanged.
  - occupancy_after = occupancy_before + placed − cleared.
  - No full line remains.
  - The score delta is P + 10L and the revision is +1.
  - The tray changes only in the consumed slot. A refill happens only when all slots are empty and deals fresh unique ids.
  - The generator changes only on a refill.
  - The status agrees with independent enumeration of legal moves.

## Limits

- Random and greedy play explore only part of the state space. These runs don't prove correctness for all possible boards.
- Long, dense boards are mostly covered by the `clearing` policy.
